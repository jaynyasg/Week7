# Building Art Direction Sample — Owner Checkpoint Artifact

**Artifact:** `building-direction-sample.png` (560×480, composed from Kenney
CC0 pieces + Fredoka SemiBold)

**Plan trace:** U1 deliverable; gates U4 world assembly. Key Technical
Decision: "Buildings from upgraded owned art — Kenney has no 2D cartoon town
buildings; campus structures get regenerated/hand-tuned owned art styled to
the Kenney palette."

## What the sample shows

A Design Build Studio facade at the target direction:

- **Base anatomy** from Kenney Background Elements `house_beige_front`
  (soft flat fills, muted warm wall, saturated roof, no heavy black outline).
- **Door** from Kenney New Platformer Pack `door_closed` (chunky, rounded,
  toy-like, oversized relative to realism — kid-scale doors read as inviting).
- **Sign band** in Fredoka SemiBold on a rounded activity-color plate —
  the DESIGN.md Door Sign pattern at building scale.
- **Soft ground shadow** instead of hard outline grounding.

## Direction spec for final building art (U4)

1. **Palette:** Kenney warm-muted walls (`#C8B59A` family) + one saturated
   activity-color accent (roof, awning, sign) per DESIGN.md career identity
   (Creative Coral for Design Build, Health Mint for the clinic, Logic Amber
   for the court).
2. **Shape language:** chunky silhouettes, rounded corners, flat fills with
   at most one shade step per surface — no gradients, no pixel art, no heavy
   ink outlines (departure from the generated kit's outline style).
3. **Signage:** every playable building carries a Fredoka sign band in its
   activity color; signage is part of the building art direction, not an
   afterthought label.
4. **Resolution:** author at native target resolution (~256–512 px tall at
   100 PPU). This sample is upscaled from a 102 px source and intentionally
   shows the softness that final art must avoid.
5. **Coherence test:** placed beside Toon Characters and New Platformer Pack
   props, a building must look like it shipped in the same Kenney pack.
